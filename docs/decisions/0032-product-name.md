# 0032 Product name: Bodha Assure

Date: 2026-11-28

## Decision

The product is called **Bodha Assure**, from the company Bodha LLC; it replaces the working name
Bodha Coder in every screen, email, PDF, legal page and current document. Repository, package,
AWS resource and tag names (`bodha-coder`, `bodha-staging-*`) stay as they are, because renaming
them would rebuild infrastructure for no visible gain, and decision records keep the name they were
written with.
